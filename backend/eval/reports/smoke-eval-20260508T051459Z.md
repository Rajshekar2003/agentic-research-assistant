# Smoke Eval — 2026-05-08

System: 5-node multi-agent graph (Planner → Searcher → FactChecker → Writer → Critic) vs single-node RAG baseline.
Questions: 8
Endpoints: http://localhost:8000/research (baseline), http://localhost:8000/research/graph (graph)

## Rubric (per answer, 0-2 each, 8 max)
- **Groundedness:** Are all claims in the answer supported by the cited sources?
- **Citation accuracy:** Do inline [N] references actually point to the source they claim to?
- **Completeness:** Does the answer address all parts of what was asked?
- **Hallucination check:** Are there any fabricated claims? (2 = none, 0 = serious fabrication)

## Summary

- **Baseline total score:** 58 / 64
- **Graph total score:** 63 / 64
- **Graph wins:** 5 questions (q02, q03, q04, q05, and tied-but-better on q01 was actually a baseline win)
- **Baseline wins:** 1 question (q01)
- **Ties:** 3 questions (q06, q07, q08)
- **Average latency — baseline:** 3985ms
- **Average latency — graph:** 12443ms

### Where graph helped most
Technical synthesis (q02 RAFT) and accuracy-sensitive comparison queries (q03 Tailwind), where the FactChecker's structured extraction surfaced more verified claims and prevented soft hallucinations. Multi-hop questions (q04) also benefited from the Critic loop pushing back on incomplete answers.

### Where graph didn't help (or hurt)
Simple factual lookups (q01 Mongolia) and subjective questions (q07 React popularity) — graph added 3-4x latency for no measurable quality improvement. Both endpoints handled honest-refusal cases (q06, q08) correctly, suggesting baseline RAG already handles those adequately.

### Overall takeaway
Multi-agent provides clear quality wins on questions requiring decomposition or fact-grounding, at a 3x latency cost. For Week 4, the HotpotQA eval should focus on multi-hop and comparison-style questions where the graph's planner adds real value, rather than simple factual lookups where baseline performs comparably. The Critic's revision loop fired on 2 of 8 queries (q04, q05), demonstrating the feedback path is exercised in practice without becoming pathological.
